Machakos County briefing

Kenya · area 5,953 km² · density 239 / km² · avg household 3.6 persons

A fast-urbanising county on Nairobi's south-eastern doorstep, Machakos has been transformed by its position astride the Nairobi–Mombasa highway and SGR line. Athi River/Mavoko has become a cement, steel and export-processing-zone manufacturing belt and a dormitory town for Nairobi commuters, while Konza Technopolis — Kenya's flagship 'smart city' project — rises on the county's plains. Machakos town itself, the old colonial-era provincial capital, retains a quieter highland character. Away from the industrial corridor, the county is Kenya's leading mango-producing area (Yatta and Mwala), and Masinga Dam on the Tana River anchors both hydropower and irrigation. Semi-arid eastern wards still depend heavily on rain-fed maize and beans, making the county a study in contrasts between booming urban growth and persistent rural vulnerability.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Industry, manufacturing & the Nairobi commuter belt
Mavoko sub-county (Athi River, Syokimau, Mlolongo) has become Kenya's cement, steel and export-processing-zone corridor, and a booming dormitory for Nairobi commuters along the SGR and highway. Konza Technopolis, the flagship national 'smart city' project, is rising on Machakos plains nearby, positioning the county as Nairobi's fastest-growing industrial and residential overflow zone.
Agriculture & horticulture
Machakos is Kenya's leading mango-producing county, concentrated in Yatta and Mwala, alongside maize, beans, pigeon peas and increasingly avocado for export markets. Most farming outside irrigated river pockets is rain-fed and vulnerable to the erratic short and long rains typical of this semi-arid stretch of Ukambani, making crop insurance and drought-tolerant seed uptake a growing county priority.
Water, energy & the Tana River
Masinga Dam, the largest of the Seven Forks hydropower reservoirs on the Tana River, sits on the Machakos–Kitui boundary and supplies a meaningful share of Kenya's grid power as well as fisheries and irrigation water locally. Elsewhere, boreholes, water pans and the Yatta furrow — one of Africa's longest human-made irrigation canals — are the backbone of rural water supply.
Urbanisation, housing & land
Rapid population growth along the Nairobi–Machakos–Mavoko axis has driven a real-estate boom, rising land values and pressure on county planning capacity. Machakos town itself, the old colonial provincial capital, is investing in stadium, park and county-government infrastructure to reposition itself as a satellite city rather than simply Nairobi's industrial backyard.

Climate risk context

Machakos County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
