Nandi County briefing

Kenya · area 2,884.5 km² · density 307 / km² · avg household 4.63 persons

Nandi occupies the western highland escarpment above the Rift Valley floor, a cool, well-watered tea county straddling roughly 1,500-2,200 m elevation. Dense smallholder tea farming under KTDA cooperatives coexists with large multinational estates around Nandi Hills, alongside dairy and maize on steep, fertile slopes. Forest reserves at Nandi Hills and Kobujoi feed rivers into the Lake Victoria basin. Nandi's global claim to fame, however, is athletics: the county has produced a remarkable concentration of the world's top distance runners, whose earnings and training camps now form a distinctive strand of the local economy alongside tea and dairy farming.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Sector deep-dive click a heading to expand

Tea economy
Nandi is among Kenya's top tea-producing counties, combining dense KTDA smallholder tea in Aldai, Chesumei and Mosop with large multinational estates around Nandi Hills. Factory gate prices and ageing bushes are the sector's recurring concerns.
Dairy & food security
Zero-grazing and pasture-based dairy supplements tea income across the highlands; maize remains the staple food crop, with production sensitive to the increasingly erratic onset of the long rains.
Forest & water catchment
The Nandi Hills escarpment and Nandi Forest reserves (South Nandi, Kobujoi) are a significant water catchment feeding rivers into the Lake Victoria basin and the Kingwal swamp sitatunga sanctuary; encroachment and illegal logging are ongoing management challenges.
Athletics & human capital
Nandi is globally renowned as the heartland of Kenyan (and world) distance running, producing a disproportionate share of elite marathoners and Olympic medallists. Athletics academies, training camps and athlete-funded community investment are a distinctive feature of the county's social economy.

Climate risk context

Nandi County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
