Meru County briefing

Kenya · area 6,936 km² · density 223 / km² · avg household 4.0 persons

Meru wraps around the northern and eastern flanks of Mt Kenya, and for most of its length is high-potential agricultural country — tea, coffee, dairy and, above all, miraa (khat): Maua, in Igembe, hosts East Africa's largest miraa market, feeding an export trade to Somalia worth billions of shillings annually. Meru town, the county HQ, anchors a dense highland economy of tea factories, banks and Mt Kenya-facing farms. Only the far northern fringe — Igembe North and parts of Tigania East, bordering Isiolo and Meru National Park — is true ASAL: semi-arid livestock country where drought occasionally pushes the area into NDMA Alert, even as the rest of the county remains firmly in Kenya's agricultural heartland.

GAM (acute malnutrition): 5–9% county-wide; up to 14% in Igembe North/Tigania East during drought years (rest of county not a nutrition hotspot)

Sector deep-dive click a heading to expand

Miraa (khat) economy
Maua, in Igembe Central, hosts East Africa's largest miraa (khat) market, the centre of a cash-crop trade worth billions of shillings annually and a major source of formal and informal employment across Igembe and Tigania. Export markets in Somalia are the trade's lifeline, and the crop's resilience to erratic rainfall makes it a preferred hedge against crop failure.
Tea, coffee & dairy farming
The Imenti and Buuri highlands around Mt Kenya's northern slopes are prime tea and coffee country, feeding numerous local factories, alongside intensive smallholder dairy farming that supplies Meru town's processors and the national milk market. This highland belt is what makes Meru, overall, one of Kenya's more food-secure and higher-income agricultural counties.
Water towers & natural resources
Mt Kenya and Imenti forests feed the rivers that irrigate Meru's highland farms and, further downstream, sustain Isiolo and Tana basin communities. Illegal logging, forest excisions and charcoal burning on the forest fringe are longstanding conservation concerns that the county and Kenya Forest Service actively police, given the basin-wide dependence on these headwaters.
Northern drylands & drought risk
Igembe North (Laare) and parts of Tigania East, bordering Isiolo and Meru National Park, are semi-arid livestock country and Meru's only NDMA-monitored ASAL fringe. Drought here can push the sub-counties into Alert status even as the rest of Meru remains agriculturally secure, making targeted rather than county-wide drought response the appropriate policy tool.

Drought history — the recurrence the index is built for

1999–2000
Severe Horn drought; emergency operations across northern Kenya and southern Ethiopia.
2005–06
Failed short rains; major livestock losses in Marsabit and Borena; catalyst period for founding of local NGOs including PACIDA (2008).
2008–09
Back-to-back poor seasons; food-insecure caseload across ASALs peaks above 3.8 million.
2010–11
Horn of Africa famine — the region's worst crisis in 60 years; famine declared in Somalia, Emergency phases across the cross-border cluster.
2016–17
Drought emergency declared in Kenya; GAM breaches 30% in parts of the north; massive livestock deaths in Borena.
2020–23
Five consecutive failed rainy seasons — worst drought in 40 years. 3.3+ million livestock deaths in southern Ethiopia; 4.4 M people on relief in Kenya's ASALs; Borena cattle herds cut by over half.
2023–24
El Niño whiplash: catastrophic floods (Isiolo among worst-hit) immediately after drought — the twin-disaster pattern.
2025–26
Failed 2025 short rains; NDMA moved 12–13 counties to Alert and 4 to Alarm by Feb 2026; 3.3 M food insecure. March 2026 rains brought partial, uneven relief; long-term recovery deficit persists.
