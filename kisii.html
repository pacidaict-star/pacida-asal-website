Kisii County briefing

Kenya · area 1,318 km² · density 961 / km² · avg household 4.3 persons

Kisii is the densely settled Gusii highlands, among Kenya's most intensively farmed rural counties, where bananas, tea, coffee, maize and dairy are grown on plots often smaller than half an acre. Fertile volcanic soils and reliable rainfall (1,500–2,000 mm) make the county highly productive per hectare despite extreme land fragmentation across generations. Tabaka's soapstone quarries and carving workshops supply a national and export handicraft trade, giving South Mugirango a distinctive artisanal identity. Kisii town itself is a major regional commercial and transport hub for the wider Gusii-Kuria-Trans Mara region. Land pressure, subdivision below economic viability and heavy youth out-migration to Nairobi and Kisumu are the county's defining social challenges.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Sector deep-dive click a heading to expand

Smallholder agriculture
Bananas, tea, coffee, maize and dairy on tiny, intensively farmed plots (often under half an acre) sustain most households; Kisii's fertile volcanic soils and reliable rainfall make it one of Kenya's most agriculturally productive counties per hectare despite extreme land fragmentation.
Soapstone & artisanal trade
Tabaka's soapstone quarries and carving workshops supply a national and export handicraft trade, providing off-farm income for thousands of artisan households and a distinctive cultural-economic identity for South Mugirango.
Land pressure & urbanisation
Kisii is among Kenya's most densely populated counties; average landholdings have shrunk across generations, driving land disputes, subdivision below economic viability, and heavy out-migration of young people to Nairobi, Kisumu and abroad.
Trade & regional commerce
Kisii town is a major regional market and transport hub for the wider Gusii-Kuria-Trans Mara region, with a large informal trade and matatu sector; boda boda and small retail absorb much of the underemployed youth population.

Climate risk context

Kisii County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
