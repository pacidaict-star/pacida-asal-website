Nakuru County briefing

Kenya · area 7,509.5 km² · density 288 / km² · avg household 3.6 persons

Nakuru sits astride the floor and eastern wall of the Great Rift Valley, Kenya's fourth most populous county and, since 2021, its newest city. Wheat, maize, potato and dairy farming on the fertile Rift floor around Njoro, Rongai and Molo make it a national breadbasket, while Naivasha's lakeside flower farms and Olkaria's geothermal fields anchor one of the country's largest horticultural export and energy economies. Nakuru city itself has boomed as a logistics, manufacturing and services hub on the Nairobi-Kampala highway. Lake Nakuru's flamingos and rhino sanctuary and Hell's Gate near Naivasha add a significant tourism dimension to an already diversified economy.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Economy & agriculture
Nakuru is the Rift Valley's commercial breadbasket — large-scale wheat, maize, potato and dairy farming across Njoro, Rongai and Molo — anchoring national food supply. Nakuru city, elevated to city status in 2021, is Kenya's fourth-largest urban economy and a fast-growing logistics and manufacturing hub on the Nairobi-Kampala corridor.
Floriculture & horticulture exports
Naivasha's lakeside flower farms are among Kenya's largest horticultural exporters, employing tens of thousands and drawing on the lake and Olkaria geothermal power. Water abstraction, lake-level decline and labour conditions on the flower estates are recurring policy debates.
Urbanisation & industry
Nakuru city's population has surged along the A104 highway, straining housing, water and sanitation; the county is a key node in Kenya's secondary-cities growth strategy alongside industrial and jua kali growth in Nakuru East.
Environment & climate
Lake Naivasha and Lake Nakuru water levels, Mau Forest Complex degradation upstream, and geothermal development at Olkaria are the county's central environmental issues; erratic rainfall increasingly disrupts the wheat/maize planting calendar on the Rift floor.

Climate risk context

Nakuru County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
