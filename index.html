Rating framework — how intervention level is decided

The Intervention Need Index (0–100) is aligned with the frameworks used by the world's main drought and food-security institutions: Kenya's NDMA drought early-warning phases, the IPC (Integrated Food Security Phase Classification) used by FAO, WFP, UNICEF, OCHA and FEWS NET, and WHO/UNICEF acute-malnutrition thresholds. Live climate signals are re-scored on every refresh; structural indicators come from the latest published assessments. Unfamiliar term? Open the Glossary in the header.

Structural vulnerability — composite of: poverty headcount (KNBS 2019/21), acute-malnutrition burden (WHO: GAM ≥15% = critical, 10–14.9% = serious), latest NDMA/IPC phase, water-access distance from NDMA bulletinsKNBS · WHO/UNICEF · NDMA monthly bulletins · IPC country analysesExpert-set 0–100 score per region, updated when new bulletins are published45%

NDMA's full indicator set also includes livestock body condition, milk production, terms of trade and MUAC screening — these are field-collected monthly and are folded into the structural score when bulletins are released. The index is a triage aid for prioritisation; deployment decisions should follow ground-truthing and joint assessments (county steering groups, Kenya Food Security Steering Group, IPC technical working groups).
