Kisumu County briefing

Kenya · area 2,010 km² · density 575 / km² · avg household 3.6 persons

Kisumu is Kenya's third-largest city and the chief Kenyan port on Lake Victoria, the historic terminus of the Kenya-Uganda Railway and a fast-repositioning regional logistics hub linking western Kenya to Uganda and South Sudan. Dunga and other beaches anchor a Nile perch, omena and tilapia fishing industry that has struggled with declining stocks, invasive water hyacinth and overfishing. Rice thrives on the Ahero and West Kano irrigation schemes, while the ageing Miwani-Muhoroni-Chemelil sugar belt remains a major smallholder cash crop despite mill and cane-payment troubles. Low-lying lakeside wards flood almost every long-rains season, and a large urban informal-trade and matatu economy anchors household livelihoods in and around the city itself.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Lake Victoria fisheries
Kisumu's Dunga and other landing sites anchor Kenya's Lake Victoria fishing industry (Nile perch, omena, tilapia); declining fish stocks from overfishing, illegal gear and invasive water hyacinth have squeezed incomes and pushed processors toward smaller catch sizes and rising imports.
Sugar & irrigated agriculture
The Ahero and West Kano irrigation schemes make Nyando and Muhoroni significant rice producers, while the Miwani-Muhoroni-Chemelil sugar belt, though beset by ageing mills and cane-payment disputes, remains a major smallholder cash crop.
Port, trade & transport
As the historic terminus of the Kenya-Uganda Railway and Lake Victoria's main Kenyan port, Kisumu is being repositioned as a regional logistics hub linking western Kenya to Uganda, South Sudan and the wider Great Lakes region, alongside a fast-growing urban service economy.
Water, flooding & environment
Low-lying lakeside wards (Nyando, Kisumu East, parts of Nyakach) flood almost every long-rains season as the Nyando and Kibos rivers overtop their banks; periodic water hyacinth blooms on the lake choke landing sites and disrupt fishing and water transport.

Climate risk context

Kisumu County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
