About this website

Kenya · ASAL Climate Watch is an unofficial live monitoring dashboard for PACIDA's operational area, built in partnership with PACIDA — it is not PACIDA's official website. It combines PACIDA's own project data with live public weather feeds to show, county by county, where drought pressure is building and where PACIDA has actually worked. For PACIDA's official programme pages, reports and contact details, visit pacida.org ↗.

About PACIDA

This dashboard is a monitoring tool for PACIDA (Pastoralist Community Initiative and Development Assistance), a Northern-Kenya/Southern-Ethiopia NGO working across Marsabit, Samburu and Isiolo counties and the Borena Zone of southern Ethiopia — drought emergency response, WASH, livestock health, education access, and cross-border peace & governance. Every area shown on this site is somewhere PACIDA actually operates; nothing here is national context or a county PACIDA doesn't work in. See the Impact Dashboard for what's been delivered on the ground, project by project, with real locations named.

How this site works

Live climate signalTemperature, rainfall, humidity and soil moisture are pulled from the Open-Meteo API and re-scored into the 0–100 Need Index every 10 minutes — the map, the county pages and the homepage stat strip all update themselves, no page reload needed.
Interactive mapEvery page pairs a live Leaflet map with the data around it. Toggle layers (heat density, drought-need shading, PACIDA interventions) from the control in the map's corner; zoom in for village- level labels.
Operational Areas mapA map-only view of all four areas with every project pinned, no surrounding dashboard chrome — useful for a quick, full-screen look. Reached from every page's header nav.
Impact DashboardThe full 236-project register behind every number here — searchable, sortable, exportable, with the real donor, theme and location named for each one.
GlossaryClick Glossary in the header on any page for plain-language definitions of NDMA, IPC, ASAL and local pastoralist terms used throughout the site.
Search & exportThe search box in the header jumps straight to any county from anywhere on the site. Most data panels also offer a CSV export of what's currently on screen.

Rating framework — how intervention level is decided

The Intervention Need Index (0–100) is aligned with the frameworks used by the world's main drought and food-security institutions: Kenya's NDMA drought early-warning phases (Marsabit, Samburu and Isiolo), the IPC (Integrated Food Security Phase Classification) used by FAO, WFP, UNICEF, OCHA and FEWS NET, and WHO/UNICEF acute-malnutrition thresholds. Live climate signals are re-scored on every refresh; structural indicators come from the latest published NDMA/IPC assessments. Unfamiliar term? Open the Glossary in the header.
