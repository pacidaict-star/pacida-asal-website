Nyandarua County briefing

Kenya · area 3,528.1 km² · density 181 / km² · avg household 3.7 persons

Nyandarua occupies the cool, high-altitude shoulder of the Aberdare Range on the eastern edge of the Rift Valley, one of Kenya's premier dairy and potato-producing counties. Small, intensively farmed holdings around Ol Kalou, Kinangop and Ol Jorok supply Nairobi's milk and fresh-produce markets, while pyrethrum survives as a legacy crop in Kipipiri. Rivers rising in the Aberdare forest feed both the Tana and Ewaso Ng'iro systems, making the county's forest boundary an important — and contested — water catchment where crop-raiding wildlife meets farmland. Ndaragwa in the north grades toward drier, Laikipia-like conditions, hinting at the climatic transition just beyond the county's edge.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Dairy economy
Nyandarua is consistently among Kenya's top milk-producing counties, its cool, well-watered highlands ideal for zero-grazing and pasture-based Friesian and Ayrshire dairying; cooperative milk coolers and processors anchor household incomes.
Potato & horticulture
A leading national potato producer, with Kinangop and Ol Kalou farms supplying Nairobi's fresh-produce markets; price volatility, certified-seed shortages and blight are recurring risks to this single-crop-dependent income stream.
Forest & water catchment
The Aberdare Range and Kipipiri forest bound the county's eastern edge, a critical water tower feeding rivers into both the Tana and Ewaso Ng'iro basins; crop-raiding by forest wildlife (elephant, buffalo) along the boundary is a persistent farmer grievance.
Climate risk to highland agriculture
Despite its high-potential status, increasingly erratic frost, hail and rainfall-onset patterns are disrupting the tightly timed potato and dairy-fodder calendar, especially in Kinangop and Ol Jorok — a slow-onset climate concern for a county not otherwise considered at risk.

Climate risk context

Nyandarua County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
