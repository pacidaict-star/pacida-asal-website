Kiambu County briefing

Kenya · area 2,449 km² · density 987 / km² · avg household 3.3 persons

Kiambu is Nairobi's northern commuter hinterland and, after Nairobi itself, Kenya's second most populous county. Former colonial 'white highlands' coffee and tea estates around Kikuyu, Limuru and Githunguri now sit alongside booming dormitory towns — Thika, Ruiru, Juja and Kikuyu — that absorb Nairobi's overspill population and much of its light industry along the Thika Superhighway corridor. This is the Kikuyu political and economic heartland, with some of the country's highest land values outside Nairobi and rapid, often unplanned urban sprawl. The Ndakaini and Thika dams, fed by Aberdare-foothill forests within the county, supply most of Nairobi's piped water — making Kiambu's upper catchments a quiet but critical national water-security asset.

GAM (acute malnutrition): Not a nutrition hotspot — standard child health surveillance applies

Industry & commuter economy
Kiambu is Nairobi's dormitory and industrial hinterland: Thika's textile, steel and vehicle-assembly plants, Ruiru's fast-growing residential-industrial belt, and the corridor along the Thika Superhighway absorb both Kiambu residents and daily commuters from across the county.
Commercial agriculture
Former colonial coffee and tea estates (Del Monte pineapples at Thika, tea in Limuru/Lari, coffee cooperatives countywide) remain economically significant even as urban sprawl converts farmland to housing plots, especially around Ruiru, Juja and Kikuyu.
Urbanisation & land
Kiambu has some of Kenya's fastest urban population growth and highest land values outside Nairobi itself; rapid, often unplanned subdivision of former farms into residential estates strains water, sewerage and road infrastructure, particularly in Ruiru, Juja and Kikuyu.
Water & environment
The Ndakaini and Thika dams, within or bordering the county, supply most of Nairobi's piped water, making Kiambu's upper-catchment forests (Aberdare foothills, Kereita) a national water-security asset; reduced forest cover in Lari and Limuru threatens dry-season flows.

Climate risk context

Kiambu County is not one of the counties on NDMA's official ASAL/drought early-warning list, so it does not receive a monthly drought-phase bulletin the way Kenya's 23 arid and semi-arid counties do. The live weather and Need Index above still update in real time — they simply carry less weight from structural drought vulnerability and more from the raw climate signal.
